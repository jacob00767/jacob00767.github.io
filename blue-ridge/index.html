Choosing materials

Wood or composite?

Both make a great deck. Here's how they compare so you can pick what fits your budget and your weekends.

Pressure-treated woodComposite
Upfront costLowerHigher
UpkeepClean, stain and seal every few yearsOccasional wash, no staining
LookClassic natural wood grainWood-look boards in many colors
Splinters & rotCan splinter, check, and rot if neglectedResists splintering, rot and insects
Best forTighter budgets and traditional styleLow maintenance over the long haul
