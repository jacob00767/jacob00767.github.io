Extensions

Two methods, matched to your hair, your lifestyle and how long you want them to last.

Hand-tied

Hand-tied wefts

Rows of fine wefts sewn onto a small beaded track. Lies flat, moves naturally and adds serious volume.

  • Best for full, even volume
  • Moved up as your hair grows
Tape-in

Tape-in extensions

Thin, lightweight panels placed close to the root. Quick to install and comfortable to wear.

  • Great for fine hair
  • Reusable at each move-up

Every extension client starts with a consultation. Leeza color-matches your hair and recommends the method and amount, and your quote is set before the install.
